# Glyph

A HUD engine for modern Paper and Velocity, based on BetterHud dev.

See [README.md](README.md) for setup and [GLYPH_PERFORMANCE.md](GLYPH_PERFORMANCE.md)
for measured results and validation limits. Glyph has no published download page yet.

Upstream: [BetterHud](https://github.com/toxicity188/BetterHud), by toxicity188 and
contributors. MIT attribution and full Git history are retained.
